# Engineering Strategy

Engineering strategy is a coherent set of technical choices and sequencing decisions that addresses an important constraint and improves the odds of a desired outcome.

## A useful shape

1. **Diagnosis:** what is true about the environment, bottleneck, and stakes?
2. **Guiding policy:** which approach and tradeoff posture will govern choices?
3. **Coherent actions:** which reinforcing investments, migrations, or constraints follow?
4. **Feedback:** which signals will tell us to adapt?

A roadmap says what may happen. A strategy explains why actions belong together and what will deliberately not be done.

## Failure patterns

- A technology preference presented as strategy
- A list of projects with no diagnosis
- Goals with no hard choices
- A target architecture with no migration or adoption model
- Metrics that measure activity rather than changed outcomes

## Connections

- [[Architecture Review]] should evaluate designs against strategic constraints, not only technical correctness.
- Lessons from [[Incident Learning]] may invalidate a strategy's assumptions or reveal a new constraint.
